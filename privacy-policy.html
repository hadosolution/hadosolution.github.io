Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HaDoSolution ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our mobile applications for Android and iOS (the "Apps") and this website. By using our Apps, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address or phone number inside our Apps.

Some information may be collected automatically by the operating system or by third-party services integrated into our Apps:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language and region settings.
  • Advertising identifiers — such as the Google Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only with your permission).
  • Usage and diagnostic data — app interactions, crash logs and performance data used to improve stability.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address by advertising partners, used to serve relevant ads.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message, which we use only to respond to you.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Apps.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • To display advertisements that help keep our Apps free.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. Third-party services

Our Apps may use the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Start.io
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics

These providers may collect and process data as described in their respective policies. Our list of authorized ad sellers is published in our app-ads.txt file.

4. Advertising and your choices

You can limit personalized advertising at any time. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID. On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track". Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

5. App permissions

Our Apps only request the permissions needed for their features (for example, camera access for a scanning feature). Each permission is requested at the time it is needed, and you can revoke it in your device settings at any time.

6. Data retention and security

Data stored by our Apps is kept on your device unless stated otherwise in the App. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hadosolution@gmail.com.
